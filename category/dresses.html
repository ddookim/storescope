How this list is built

StoreScope crawls the public /products.json feed of 58 curated independent DTC brands every Sunday night. New items published in the last 30 days are grouped by product_type (Shopify's native taxonomy), deduplicated across brands (perceptual hash + title similarity), and surfaced by first-appearance freshness. Vendor and category diversity caps prevent any single brand from dominating the weekly digest.

The full list of new dresses — and every other category — is delivered as a single Monday-morning email. No dashboards to check.
