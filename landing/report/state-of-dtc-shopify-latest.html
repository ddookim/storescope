Price distribution

The top-20 digest spans $12-$279 with a median of $60. This reflects the curated seed skewing toward mid-to-premium DTC (no Aliexpress dropshippers). Sub-$20 items are typically consumables or accessories (Liquid Death cans, Ridge card-holder replacements); $200+ items are outerwear or premium skincare kits (True Botanicals, Summer Fridays sets).

Freshness

The average product in this week's top 20 is 1.9 days old, with the newest at 0.2 days. The pipeline enforces a 30-day sliding window; anything older is dropped. If a brand went dark for a week, they drop off. If a brand launches a Monday drop, they surface in the next Sunday crawl.

Methodology

StoreScope crawls the public /products.json feed of each curated brand every Sunday night (UTC). No authenticated data, no private catalogs, no scraping of ad libraries — only the same feed every Shopify store makes publicly available. Deduplication uses perceptual image hash (pHash) + title similarity to collapse near-identical products across brands (e.g. white unisex t-shirts). Categories use Shopify's native product_type field (which is why 81 items land in "Uncategorized" — brands vary in taxonomy discipline).

What this report is NOT:

  • Not a "trend prediction" — no ML, no ad-spend spy, no revenue estimate.
  • Not exhaustive of the DTC universe — 58 brands is a curated slice, not "all Shopify."
  • Not a leading indicator of what will "go viral" — it's a lagging indicator of what these brands chose to ship.
