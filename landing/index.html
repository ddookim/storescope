FAQ

Common questions.

What's in the Monday email?

The 20 newest products launched in the past week across all 58 brands we watch. Each row: product name, brand, category, price, launch date, and a link to buy from the original store. That's it.

Which brands do you watch?

58 established DTC brands on Shopify — Everlane, Gymshark, Allbirds, Ridge, Beardbrand, Casper, Colourpop, Glossier, Harry's, Liquid Death, Magic Spoon, Outdoor Voices, Parachute Home, Supergoop, Taylor Stitch, and 43 more. Full list: browse them at /brands.

What does it cost?

The Monday email is free — enter your email, get the digest. Pro ($19/month) adds API access, CSV export, Storm Score momentum ranking, 30-day product history, and unlimited X-Ray lookups. 7-day free trial, no card, cancel anytime.

Is monitoring competitor stores legal?

Yes. We read publicly listed products via Shopify's open /products.json endpoint — the same data any browser sees. No auth bypass, no private data, robots.txt respected, and any merchant can opt out instantly by emailing optout@storescope.com.

How do I unsubscribe?

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. No account, no login, no forms. If you're a Pro subscriber, manage or cancel from the Paddle billing portal linked in your welcome email.
