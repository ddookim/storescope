Refund Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with StoreScope within the first 14 days after your initial billing, contact us for a full refund. No questions asked.

After 14 Days

After the initial 14-day post-billing period, subscriptions are non-refundable. You may cancel at any time to prevent future charges — cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

How to Request a Refund

Email support@storescope.com with your account email and reason. Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days.

Exceptions

Refunds will not be issued for: accounts suspended due to Terms of Service violations, or requests made after the 14-day post-billing window.

Cancellation

Cancel anytime from your account dashboard. No cancellation fees. Access continues until the end of the paid period.
