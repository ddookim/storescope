StoreScope vs Store Leads — Which One Actually Fits Your Workflow?

Short answer: Store Leads is ecommerce store database (shopify + other platforms). StoreScope is a weekly email digest of 58 curated independent DTC brands. They solve different problems — pick based on what half of the workflow you need.

What Store Leads actually is

Store Leads is a B2B data platform: filter millions of ecom stores by tech stack, apps installed, country, install date, and export CSVs for outbound sales. Their SEO moat is the 'State of Shopify' report and a free Chrome extension used as a top-of-funnel.

What StoreScope actually is

StoreScope crawls 58 hand-picked DTC Shopify brands every Sunday and sends you a Monday-morning email with what's new — sorted by first-appearance freshness, grouped by category (Bottoms, Sweaters, Denim, Skincare, etc.), with vendor and category diversity enforced. No trend prediction, no ML magic, no ad spy. Just: "here's what the curated set launched this week."

Pick Store Leads if…

You sell to Shopify merchants (an app, an agency, a service) and need lead lists filtered by tech signals. You need CSV export at scale and a $75+/mo budget is trivial compared to your ACV.

Pick StoreScope if…

You are a merchant, sourcing agent, or brand operator — not a B2B salesperson targeting merchants. You don't need CSVs of thousands of prospects. You want to see what a curated DTC slice launched this week, delivered as an email, for $19/mo.

They aren't drop-in replacements

StoreScope is not a lead-generation tool. If you're selling something to Shopify merchants, Store Leads is the correct tool and we're not it.
