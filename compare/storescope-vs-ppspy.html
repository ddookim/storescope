StoreScope vs PPSpy — Which One Actually Fits Your Workflow?

Short answer: PPSpy is ai-branded product research + tracking tool. StoreScope is a weekly email digest of 58 curated independent DTC brands. They solve different problems — pick based on what half of the workflow you need.

What PPSpy actually is

PPSpy positions itself as an AI-powered product tracker for dropshippers — 'find winning products 10x profitable' is the pitch. Their SEO play is 20+ programmatic 'X alternative' pages (Ecomhunt alternative, Niche Scraper alternative, etc.) that capture bottom-funnel competitor searches.

What StoreScope actually is

StoreScope crawls 58 hand-picked DTC Shopify brands every Sunday and sends you a Monday-morning email with what's new — sorted by first-appearance freshness, grouped by category (Bottoms, Sweaters, Denim, Skincare, etc.), with vendor and category diversity enforced. No trend prediction, no ML magic, no ad spy. Just: "here's what the curated set launched this week."

Pick PPSpy if…

You want a broad 'winning products' feed with AI-flagged trend signals and you accept dropship-heavy source stores as valid signal. You want tracking with alerts, not a weekly digest.

Pick StoreScope if…

You want DTC brand signal, not dropship signal — the two source pools behave differently. You've been burned by AI-labeled 'winners' that were actually saturated. You prefer honest curation ('these 58 brands, sorted by freshness') over black-box AI ranking.

They aren't drop-in replacements

PPSpy covers a much wider surface of dropship-adjacent stores. If your buying strategy is high-volume, low-margin dropshipping, PPSpy's signal pool is closer to your reality than ours.
