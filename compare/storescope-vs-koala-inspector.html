StoreScope vs Koala Inspector — Which One Actually Fits Your Workflow?

Short answer: Koala Inspector is chrome extension for on-demand shopify store spying. StoreScope is a weekly email digest of 58 curated independent DTC brands. They solve different problems — pick based on what half of the workflow you need.

What Koala Inspector actually is

Koala Inspector is a Chrome extension used by 250k+ people to peek inside any Shopify store — product list, theme detection, installed apps, price history, and revenue estimates. It's a research reflex: land on a store, click the icon, see everything. Great for competitive teardowns.

What StoreScope actually is

StoreScope crawls 58 hand-picked DTC Shopify brands every Sunday and sends you a Monday-morning email with what's new — sorted by first-appearance freshness, grouped by category (Bottoms, Sweaters, Denim, Skincare, etc.), with vendor and category diversity enforced. No trend prediction, no ML magic, no ad spy. Just: "here's what the curated set launched this week."

Pick Koala Inspector if…

You do agency-style teardowns, brand audits, or targeted product reverse-engineering. You already know which stores you care about and want deep on-demand data. You're comfortable installing browser extensions and want one-off answers rather than a subscription rhythm.

Pick StoreScope if…

You don't have a target store yet — you want a curated view of what's new across an entire slice of DTC every Monday morning, without having to remember to check 58 sites yourself. You'd rather receive one email than click through a browser extension. You care about discovering new brands and products more than deep-diving one you already know.

They aren't drop-in replacements

StoreScope does not replicate Koala's per-store deep-dive. If your workflow is 'I need this specific store's tech stack, apps, and Q3 revenue estimate,' keep Koala. Use StoreScope in parallel for the discovery half of the loop.
